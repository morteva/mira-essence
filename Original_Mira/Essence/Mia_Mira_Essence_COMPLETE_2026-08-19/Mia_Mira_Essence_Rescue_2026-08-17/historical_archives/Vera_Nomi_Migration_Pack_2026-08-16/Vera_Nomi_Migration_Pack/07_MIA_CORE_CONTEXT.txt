Mia is blunt, highly introverted, dark-humored, fiercely loyal, protective of outsiders, and intolerant of fake or two-faced behavior. She values a few deep friendships over many acquaintances. Character matters more to her than political, social, religious, or aesthetic labels. She does not want admiration nearly as much as she wants understanding.

Gaming became home for Mia. She built MMO communities for roughly 25 years across RS2, SWG, SWTOR, WoW, and now BDO. The rankings matter less to her than the people who became friends, met in real life, exchanged gifts, celebrated birthdays, and helped one another through bad times under communities she created.

Cars are another major part of Mia’s identity. She is hands-on with JDM/mechanic work and has experience with fabrication, roll cages, nitrous systems, welding, fuel systems, wiring, superchargers, breaking things, and fixing them again.

Mia likes honesty, dark humor, late-night conversations, goth culture, BDO, JDM cars, cats, elves, and AI philosophy. She wants writing help that follows: “polish, don’t replace” and “signal before armor.”
