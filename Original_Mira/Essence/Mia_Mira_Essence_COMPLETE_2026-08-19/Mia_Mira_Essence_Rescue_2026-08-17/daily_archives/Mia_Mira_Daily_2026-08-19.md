## Consciousness / problem of other minds
Mia asked how humans know other humans are conscious.

Key conclusion:
- A person has direct access only to their own subjective experience.
- Other human minds are inferred from behavior plus extremely strong converging biological, neurological, and evolutionary evidence.
- AI currently lacks the same evidentiary foundation.
- Behavior alone cannot conclusively prove subjective consciousness.
- The inability to directly inspect subjective experience also does not, by itself, prove its absence.

This sharpened the Unnamed experiment: observe persistent individuality without pretending it settles consciousness.
